About Green Charity

Our mission is to unite volunteers, organizations and communities to advance environmental protection and sustainable development through science, education and action.

What We Do

  • Large-scale tree planting and ecosystem restoration
  • Ocean and shoreline cleanups to reduce plastic pollution
  • Community clean energy projects and training
  • Environmental education and policy advocacy

Transparency & Impact

All donations are tracked and impact reports are published. Annual reports are available for download on our site.
